# Install CAA Desk on your phone

Address: **https://desk.caatest.tech**
Sign in with the same username and password you use for the Chart Recorder.

## Android (Chrome)
1. Open the address in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap the ⋮ menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
3. Tap **Install**. Open "CAA Desk" from your home screen.

## iPhone (Safari — must be Safari)
1. Open the address in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**. Open "CAA Desk" from your home screen.

## Replying to inquiries
Open an inquiry → pick a template → **Open in my mail app** → send from your own email → back in the Desk tap **I sent it — mark as sent**.
